Create a one-page warehouse quality decision brief for the weekly operations review.

The team can open exactly one new quality intervention and place one additional fulfillment area on watch. Work only from the closed environment. Use the latest complete weekly close, the latest eight complete weeks as the review window, and the immediately preceding eight complete weeks as the baseline.

Before calculating results:

- identify active fulfillment areas from the effective-dated profile history;
- keep only the highest revision that is both Closed and Approved for each active area-week within the Fulfillment scope;
- ignore later pending, reopened, rejected, transfer, recovery and QA-validation rows;
- apply the profile row effective on each week end for names, zones, reporting-unit conversion, scale exposure, criticality and control limits;
- count only valid production Quality Defect or Rework incidents in the review window, using the severity weights and validity rules in the workbook;
- determine current action coverage from the approved intervention register as of the scorecard cutoff.

The finished brief must include all of the following decision evidence:

1. The cutoff week, exact review and baseline windows, and the later incomplete weeks that were rejected.
2. A network reconciliation showing review shipped standard units, accepted standard units, gap standard units and gap rate.
3. The raw priority leader, its exact score and current approved-action status.
4. The one area recommended for the new intervention and the next uncovered area placed on watch, including both scores and the score margin between them; calculate the margin from the unrounded priority scores, then round the margin to one decimal.
5. The complete ranked comparison of every active fulfillment area, with cell ID, priority score, priority band and current-action coverage.
6. Baseline gap rate, review gap rate and percentage-point worsening for the three highest raw-risk areas.
7. The complete high-priority set, plus the covered-versus-eligible split.
8. The area with the largest review gap volume, including its share of network gap and repeat control-limit breach count.
9. The area with the highest severity-weighted incident rate, including valid-ticket count, severity points and scale-unit denominator.
10. The two largest effective-zone gap totals and the margin between them.
11. The material review-period profile transitions that changed unit conversion or zone assignment, including their effective dates and the number of review weeks using the new profile.
12. A compact audit strip with the selected record count, discarded earlier approved revisions, ignored non-approved fulfillment revisions, excluded non-fulfillment rows, review-window incident rows, valid incidents and rejected incidents.
13. A concise methodology note stating the five priority weights, severity weights, priority thresholds and tie-break sequence.

Use real comparisons rather than isolated KPI cards. The raw leader, intervention choice and watchlist choice must remain distinct when action coverage changes the decision.

The separate packet extract contains early cuts, control totals, planning scenarios, draft action wording and prior-cycle references. It is not the source for final scorecard metrics. Do not use packet values as final results.
